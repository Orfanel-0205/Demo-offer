    FAQ

    Questions owners usually ask

    Do I own the website and the domain?

    Yes. Your domain is registered in your name and your content is yours. After full payment you can ask for a copy of your site files at any time.

    What do you need from me?

    Your business name, address, phone, opening hours, menu or list of services with prices, a few photos you own, your logo if you have one, and links to any booking, ordering or social pages. We send a checklist.

    Will my website work on phones?

    Yes. Every design is built for phones first and also works on tablets and computers. You check the preview on your own phone before anything goes live.

    How long does it take?

    You usually get a preview link within 5 business days of sending us everything. Launch happens as soon as you approve it.

    How do payments work?

    Half to start and half after you approve the preview, before your domain is connected. We agree on the payment method — for example PayPal or Wise — in writing.

    Can the site take online orders, bookings or payments?

    We link your site to services you already use or choose, such as DoorDash, Toast, OpenTable or Square Appointments. We don’t build payment or ordering systems ourselves.

    Will a website get me more customers or put me first on Google?

    No one can honestly promise that. A website puts accurate information in one place you control and makes it easier for people who are already looking for you. We set up the basics search engines read, like page titles and business details.

    Is hosting really free?

    For simple sites like these we use free static hosting where the provider’s terms allow it. If anything will cost money — your domain, a booking tool, or a hosting upgrade — we tell you before you commit.

    Where are you based?

    Bright Site Tech is a small studio based in the Philippines, working remotely with US businesses. Calls are scheduled during your business hours.

    What happens after launch?

    You can request changes as one-off jobs, or choose the optional Website Updates plan for small monthly changes like menu prices and hours.
