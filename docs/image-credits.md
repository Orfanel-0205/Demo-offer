# Image credits

All photos in the sample designs are released under **CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)** on Wikimedia Commons, so no attribution is legally required. They're credited here as a courtesy. Each one was resized, cropped and converted to WebP.

The photos are **placeholders**. They don't show Harbor & Hearth Kitchen or Copperline Auto Care (both fictional), or any real business's food, staff or premises as theirs. For a client's website, replace them with photos the client owns or is licensed to use.
