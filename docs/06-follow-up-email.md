# 6 · Follow-up email

Send this **the same day** to an owner who agreed on the phone to receive the sample. Replace everything in `[brackets]`. Keep it short and plain. No images or attachments, which often land in spam.

---

**Subject:** The sample website I mentioned, [Your name]

*(Alternatives: "Sample restaurant website for [Restaurant name]" · "Following up on our call today")*

---

Hi [Owner name],

Thanks for taking my call today about [Restaurant name]. As promised, here's the sample website I mentioned:

**[DEMO LINK]**

It's built for a made-up restaurant, Harbor & Hearth Kitchen, so all the details are placeholders. It shows how a site for [Restaurant name] could look: your menu as easy-to-read text, your hours, a map with directions, a tap-to-call button, and links to the services you already use, like [DoorDash / OpenTable / your Instagram]. It's designed for phones first.

For my first couple of restaurant clients I'm offering a **one-page website for $149, one-time**, including one round of changes. The domain name is separate and registered in your name. Before any work starts, we'd agree on exactly what's included in writing.

**Next step:** if you'd like to explore it, reply with a good time for a 10-minute call, or just send over your current menu and I'll outline what your site would include. If it's not a fit right now, no problem at all.

Thanks again,

[Your name]
RestaurantLaunch: websites for independent restaurants
[Your business email] · [Your phone/WhatsApp, optional]
[Your business mailing address]

*If you'd rather not hear from me again, just reply "no thanks" and I won't follow up.*

## Short text-message version

Only if the owner **asked** you to text them:

> Hi [Owner name], it's [Your name] from our call today. Here's the sample restaurant website: [DEMO LINK]. It's a made-up restaurant, so your site would use your own menu, photos and hours. Intro price is $149 one-time (domain separate). Any questions, just reply here. Thanks!

---

## Checklist before you hit send

- [ ] Their name and the restaurant name are spelled correctly.
- [ ] The demo link opens on your phone.
- [ ] Price matches `site/agency.config.js` (and what you said on the call).
- [ ] No promises about more customers, orders, revenue or Google rankings.
- [ ] Your real name, a working reply address, and an easy way to opt out are included.
- [ ] A physical mailing address for your business is in the signature. US anti-spam rules (CAN-SPAM) expect one in commercial email.
