# 4 · Demo walkthrough script

Use this while showing the demo to a restaurant owner on a phone: on a video call (share your screen), in person, or as narration over the demo video. It takes about **90 seconds**. Keep it conversational and stop whenever they ask a question.

**Before you start:** open the demo link, scroll to the top, and close other tabs. On a video call, hold the phone steady or share your phone screen.

---

### 1. Set expectations (10 sec)

> "This is a sample site I built for a made-up restaurant called Harbor & Hearth, so you can see how it works. Everything on it is placeholder content. Your site would have your own menu, photos and hours, all approved by you."

### 2. First screen (15 sec)
*Point at the name, the two buttons and the green "Open now" badge.*

> "When a customer taps your link, from Google, Instagram or a text from a friend, this is the first thing they see: your name, a photo, and the two things people look for most, the menu and directions. This little badge shows whether you're open right now, based on your hours."

### 3. Menu (20 sec)
*Tap* **Explore Our Menu**, *then tap* **Desserts**, *then* **All**.

> "Here's the menu as real text, not a blurry photo, so it's easy to read on any phone. Customers can jump to a category with one tap. When your prices change, you send me the update and I change it."

### 4. Story and photos (10 sec)
*Scroll through About and swipe the gallery.*

> "This part tells your story in your words, with your own photos."

### 5. Ordering and reservations (15 sec)
*Scroll to* **Order & reserve**, *tap one demo button.*

> "If you already use something like DoorDash, Toast or OpenTable, these buttons link straight to your page there. The site doesn't replace those services or take payments. It just helps people find them. If you don't use any, this section simply doesn't appear."

### 6. Visit and contact (10 sec)
*Scroll to* **Visit & contact**, *point at Call, Directions and the hours table.*

> "Address, hours for every day, a call button that opens the phone dialer, and directions in Google Maps. Today's hours are highlighted."

### 7. Always-there buttons (5 sec)
*Point at the bottom bar.*

> "On phones, Menu, Call and Directions stay at the bottom wherever they scroll."

### 8. Close with a question, not a pitch (10 sec)

> "So that's the idea: everything a customer needs in one place, on a site you own. Does something like this make sense for [restaurant name], or is there something you'd want it to do differently?"

If they're interested, show the pricing section at the bottom (or tap **See pricing** at the top) and walk through the three options. See [guide 5](05-cold-call-script.md) for answers to common questions.

---

## Things to avoid saying

- ❌ "This will bring you more customers / more orders / more revenue."
- ❌ "This will get you to the top of Google."
- ❌ "Everyone needs a website." (Let them decide.)
- ✅ Say instead: "It puts your menu, hours and location in one place that you control, so people who are already looking for you can find what they need."

## If you're sending the video instead

Use `videos/harbor-hearth-phone-demo.mp4` (vertical). It works well on Messenger, WhatsApp and Instagram. Add one line of context when you send it, for example: *"Here's the 1-minute sample I mentioned. It's a made-up restaurant, so everything would be replaced with your own menu and photos."*
