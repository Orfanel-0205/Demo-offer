# 6 · Demo walkthrough script

Use this while showing a sample design to an owner: on a video call (share your phone screen), in person, or as narration over a demo video. Each walkthrough takes about **90 seconds**. Keep it conversational and stop whenever they ask a question.

**Before you start:** open the right sample, scroll to the top and close other tabs.

- Restaurants, cafés, bakeries → `…/demos/restaurant/`
- Auto shops, mechanics, trades and other service businesses → `…/demos/auto-repair/`

---

## Opening (both samples, 10 seconds)

> "This is a sample site I built for a made-up [restaurant / auto shop] so you can see how it works. Everything on it is placeholder content. Your site would have your own [menu / services], photos and hours, all approved by you."

## 🍽️ Restaurant sample: Harbor & Hearth Kitchen

1. **First screen** *(point at the name, the two buttons and the green badge)*
   > "When a customer taps your link, from Google, Instagram or a text from a friend, this is what they see first: your name, a photo, and the two things people look for most, the menu and directions. This badge shows whether you're open right now, based on your hours."
2. **Menu** *(tap* **Explore Our Menu**, *then* **Desserts**, *then* **All***)*
   > "Here's the menu as real text, not a blurry photo, so it's easy to read on any phone. Customers can jump to a category with one tap. When your prices change, it's a quick update."
3. **Story and photos** *(scroll About, swipe the gallery)*
   > "This part tells your story in your words, with your own photos."
4. **Order & reserve** *(tap a demo button)*
   > "If you already use something like DoorDash, Toast or OpenTable, these buttons link straight to your page there. The site doesn't replace those services or take payments."
5. **Visit** *(point at Call, Directions, hours)*
   > "Address, hours for every day, a call button and directions in Google Maps. Today is highlighted."

## 🔧 Auto repair sample: Copperline Auto Care

1. **First screen** *(point at the call button and the "Open now" badge)*
   > "The first thing a driver sees is your name, what you do, a big call button and whether you're open right now, in your time zone."
2. **Services & prices** *(tap* **See services & prices**, *then filter* **Brakes & Suspension***)*
   > "Your services with starting prices, if you want to show them. People can filter by the kind of job. If you'd rather not list prices, we can say 'call for a quote' instead."
3. **About the shop** *(scroll to the highlights)*
   > "This is where you explain how you work, like written estimates before any work starts. Only things that are true for your shop."
4. **Book a service** *(tap* **Request a quote***)*
   > "Customers can book through the tool you already use, send a quote request that comes to your email, or text you."
5. **Visit** *(point at Call the shop and hours)*
   > "Hours, address, one-tap calling and directions."

## Closing (both samples)

*(Point at the bottom bar.)*
> "On phones, [Menu / Services], Call and Directions stay at the bottom wherever they scroll."

Then ask, don't pitch:
> "So that's the idea: everything a customer needs in one place, on a site you own. Does something like this make sense for [business name], or is there something you'd want it to do differently?"

If they're interested, scroll to the panel at the bottom (or tap **See pricing** at the top) to show the packages, or open your Bright Site Tech page. Answers to common questions: [guide 7](07-cold-call-script.md).

---

## Things to avoid saying

- ❌ "This will bring you more customers / more orders / more revenue."
- ❌ "This will get you to the top of Google."
- ❌ "Everyone needs a website." (Let them decide.)
- ✅ Say instead: "It puts your information in one place that you control, so people who are already looking for you can find what they need."
