# 9 · Client onboarding checklist

Collect all of this **before** you start building. Send the owner this list (or turn it into a simple Google Form), and don't start until the essentials (★) are in.

Business: ____________________ Type: ____________ Date started: __________
Package: ☐ Starter $149 ☐ Business $249 ☐ + Website Updates $39/mo

---

## 1. Business and authority

- [ ] ★ **Official business name**, spelled exactly as it should appear (and any short name)
- [ ] ★ **Authorized contact**: the person who can approve content and pay (name, role, email, phone)
- [ ] Confirmation that this person is allowed to make decisions for the business (owner, partner or manager with approval)
- [ ] Preferred way to communicate (email, WhatsApp, text, calls) and best times
- [ ] Logo file (PNG or SVG, the largest version they have)
- [ ] Brand colors or a photo of their sign/menu to match (see the industry playbook, guide 5)

## 2. Location and hours

- [ ] ★ **Street address** exactly as on their Google Business Profile
- [ ] ★ **Regular opening hours** for every day, including split shifts and closed days
- [ ] Time zone (confirm the state/city)
- [ ] Holiday or seasonal hours notice, if any
- [ ] Parking, accessibility or entrance notes, only what they confirm

## 3. Menu or services, and prices

- [ ] ★ **Current menu or list of services** (PDF, photo of the printed menu or price board, or a typed list)
- [ ] ★ **Prices** for every item, confirmed as current
- [ ] Category names and order (e.g. Starters, Mains, Desserts · Maintenance, Brakes, Diagnostics)
- [ ] Labels such as dietary tags (V, GF) or "same day", **only those the owner confirms in writing**
- [ ] Allergy/disclaimer wording they want (e.g. "Please tell your server about allergies.")
- [ ] Items to leave out (seasonal, discontinued, alcohol, jobs they don't want to advertise)
- [ ] Whether to show prices at all, starting prices ("From $69"), or "call for a quote"

## 4. Photos and usage rights

- [ ] ★ **Approved photographs**: food, interior, exterior, team (5–10 is plenty)
- [ ] ★ Written confirmation that the business **owns the photos or has the right** to use them online (e.g. they took them, or the photographer agreed)
- [ ] Permission from any recognizable people in the photos (staff or guests)
- [ ] Which photo they want as the main (hero) image
- [ ] ⚠ Don't use photos from Google, Yelp, delivery apps or other websites without proof of permission

## 5. Phone and email

- [ ] ★ **Phone number** for the call button (which number customers should call)
- [ ] Email address to show publicly, **or** confirmation that no email should be shown
- [ ] Private contact email for you to use (not published)

## 6. Existing online profiles

- [ ] Google Business Profile link
- [ ] Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Yelp, X links they want shown
- [ ] Anything they do **not** want linked

## 7. Ordering, booking and quotes

- [ ] Online ordering link (their own Toast/Square/ChowNow/Clover page), if any
- [ ] Reservation or appointment-booking link (OpenTable, Resy, Square Appointments, Calendly, shop software…), if any
- [ ] Business package: who should receive quote/inquiry form messages, and which questions to ask (Google Form or Formspree, see guide 2)
- [ ] Delivery store links (DoorDash/Uber Eats/Grubhub/other), if any
- [ ] Confirmation they're authorized to link to each of these, and that each link is current
- [ ] Note: you link **out** to these services. No payments, orders or bookings are processed by the website itself.

## 8. Domain and hosting

- [ ] ★ Do they already own a domain? ☐ Yes: `______________` ☐ No
- [ ] If yes: registrar name, and how DNS will be updated (owner on a call / registrar's delegate-access feature)
- [ ] If no: domain they want (check availability together); they buy it in **their own name and account**
- [ ] Who pays for the domain renewal (default: the client) and the renewal date
- [ ] Hosting arrangement agreed (e.g. free static hosting on your Render account; Render's free workspace includes a limited number of custom domains, so explain any small extra cost)
- [ ] Any existing website that will be replaced, and who controls it

## 9. Content approval

- [ ] ★ Owner reviewed the **preview link** on their phone
- [ ] ★ **Written approval** of all content: name, menu, prices, hours, address, phone, links, photos, About text ("Approved, please launch" by email is fine)
- [ ] One revision round used? ☐ Not yet ☐ Used on: ______
- [ ] Date approved: __________

## 10. Scope and payment terms (in writing before work begins)

- [ ] ★ **Package and price** confirmed in writing (email or a simple agreement)
- [ ] ★ **What's included** (sections, number of revision rounds) and **what's not** (online ordering, payments, ads, custom backend, domain cost, logo design, photography, copywriting beyond the agreed sections)
- [ ] ★ **Payment terms**: suggested 50% before starting and 50% after approval, before the site goes live on their domain
- [ ] Payment method agreed (e.g. PayPal, Wise, Payoneer). Check each service's current fees for international payments.
- [ ] **Timeline**: e.g. first preview within 5 business days of receiving all ★ items
- [ ] **Ownership**: the client owns their content and domain; after full payment they may receive a copy of the site files
- [ ] If **Website Updates ($39/month)** is chosen, agree in writing:
  - [ ] Number of small update requests included per month (suggested starting point: **up to 3**)
  - [ ] What counts as "small" (e.g. menu/price/hours changes, swapping a photo, a short text edit)
  - [ ] Response time (suggested: **within 2 business days**, Philippine working hours)
  - [ ] How to cancel (e.g. any time, effective at the end of the paid month)
  - [ ] No "unlimited" requests and no guaranteed uptime. Hosting is provided by a third party.

---

## Handover (at launch)

- [ ] Live link on their domain tested on a phone
- [ ] Owner has added the website link to Google Business Profile and social bios
- [ ] Handover note sent: what's on the site, how to request changes, renewal dates, who to contact
- [ ] Final invoice paid
- [ ] Ask (don't pressure) whether they'd be willing to give feedback you could quote later, **with their permission** and in their own words
