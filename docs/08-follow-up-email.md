# 8 · Follow-up email

Send this **the same day** to an owner who agreed on the phone to receive a sample. Replace everything in `[brackets]`. Keep it short and plain. No attachments, which often land in spam.

---

**Subject:** The sample website I mentioned, [Your name] from Bright Site Tech

*(Alternatives: "Sample website for [Business name]" · "Following up on our call today")*

---

Hi [Owner name],

Thanks for taking my call today about [Business name]. As promised, here's the sample website I mentioned:

**[SAMPLE LINK: …/demos/restaurant/ or …/demos/auto-repair/]**

It's built for a made-up [restaurant / auto shop], so all the details are placeholders. It shows how a site for [Business name] could look: [your menu as easy-to-read text / your services with starting prices], your hours, directions, a tap-to-call button, and links to the tools you already use, like [DoorDash / OpenTable / your booking page]. It's designed for phones first.

My **Starter website is $149, one-time**, and the **Business website is $249** with a more custom design and a quote or inquiry form. The domain name is separate and registered in your name. Before any work starts, we'd agree on exactly what's included, in writing. You can see the packages here: **[YOUR WEBSITE LINK]#packages**

**Next step:** if you'd like to explore it, reply with a good time for a 10-minute call, or just send over your current [menu / list of services] and I'll outline what your site would include. If it's not a fit right now, no problem at all.

Thanks again,

[Your name]
Bright Site Tech · websites for local businesses
[Your website link] · [Your business email] · [Phone/WhatsApp, optional]
[Your business mailing address]

*If you'd rather not hear from me again, just reply "no thanks" and I won't follow up.*

## Short text-message version

Only if the owner **asked** you to text them:

> Hi [Owner name], it's [Your name] from Bright Site Tech, from our call today. Here's the sample website: [SAMPLE LINK]. It's a made-up business, so yours would use your own details and photos. Starter is $149 one-time (domain separate). Any questions, just reply here. Thanks!

---

## Checklist before you hit send

- [ ] Their name and business name are spelled correctly.
- [ ] You picked the right sample for their industry, and the link opens on your phone.
- [ ] Prices match `site/agency.config.js` and what you said on the call.
- [ ] No promises about more customers, orders, revenue or Google rankings.
- [ ] Your real name, a working reply address, and an easy way to opt out are included.
- [ ] A physical mailing address for your business is in the signature. US anti-spam rules (CAN-SPAM) expect one in commercial email.
- [ ] Add your logo to your Gmail signature: `brand/png/email-signature-logo.png`.
